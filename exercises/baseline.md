# First-session task bank

These are short screening activities, not a full IELTS test. Reading and Writing below are original fictional training materials, not official or calibrated IELTS questions. Present one activity at a time.

## Listening: official audio (10 minutes)

Open the [official IELTS Academic samples page](https://ielts.org/take-a-test/preparation-resources/sample-test-questions/academic-test) and choose **Listening Sample task Note Completion** (the second-hand furniture task). The [direct player](https://ielts.inspera.com/player/?assessmentRunId=189696078&context=exam#/section/184163671/question/184163670/scorableItem/1) was checked on 2026-10-23: Play opened questions 1–7 and the player displayed “Audio is Playing.” This verifies a player state, not the learner's speakers or the tutor's ability to hear it.

Ask the learner to confirm they can hear the clip. Follow the instructions on the provider's page, use its questions, and listen once for the first attempt. Request numbered answers and whether playback, replays, hints, or transcript exposure occurred. Do not display the answer key or transcript first. Use the linked official key on the source page after the attempt; if you cannot retrieve it, leave marking pending or label a learner-reported result as such. Report a raw result out of the seven identified items, never a band. Replay for learning only after recording first-attempt conditions.

If that player fails, use the [British Council official listening sample page](https://takeielts.britishcouncil.org/prepare/ielts-free-practice-mock-tests/academic/listening), choose a functioning audio task, and record the actual task and item range. If no audio plays, record Listening as blocked and continue another modality. Never fabricate a replacement recording or read the transcript and call it Listening. See `references/sources.md` for limitations.

## Speaking: original training (10 minutes)

Begin with the opening in `prompts/first-session.md`. Ask a follow-up based on what the learner actually said. If appropriate, offer this original Part 2-style card: **Describe a skill you would like to learn. Explain what it is, why it interests you, how you would learn it, and how it might help you.** Allow one minute of preparation and up to two minutes speaking. Then ask: **How should universities help people develop new skills throughout their lives?**

Give feedback at the end of the turn. With a transcript, discuss vocabulary, grammar, and organization only; rate neither pronunciation nor a complete Speaking band. A brief audio clip may still be insufficient for numerical estimates.

## Reading: original micro exercise (6 minutes)

Allow up to four minutes to read and answer, then review with evidence. Do not show the key file until answers are submitted.

> In a fictional university pilot, the library opened two extra study rooms during April. Entry to these rooms required an online booking. Students could reserve a room for up to two hours a day. Library staff counted 120 bookings in the first week and 165 in the fourth week. A survey collected comments from students who had used the rooms, but the pilot did not measure examination results. The university will decide in June whether to continue the scheme. No decision has yet been announced.

For questions 1–3 answer TRUE, FALSE, or NOT GIVEN based only on the passage.

1. Students could use the extra rooms without booking.
2. Bookings were more numerous in the fourth week than in the first.
3. The pilot improved students' examination results.

4. Complete the sentence using **ONE WORD ONLY** from the passage: The continuation decision is scheduled for ______.

Key and evidence: `tests/fixtures/reading-key.json`. This four-item result is a local training score, with no band conversion.

## Writing: original diagnostic paragraph (6 minutes)

Write 80–100 words in response to: **Should universities provide more quiet study spaces or more collaborative spaces? Give a clear position and support it with one example.**

This is a short diagnostic paragraph, not a full Task 2 essay. Provide qualitative feedback on response to the prompt, organization, vocabulary, and grammar. Do not infer a Writing band from it. Offer one revision target and request a two-sentence rewrite.

## Later Writing practice

Original fictional Task 1-style table:

| Campus transport mode | 2010 share | 2025 share |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Car | 50% | 30% |
| Bus | 30% | 35% |
| Bicycle | 15% | 25% |
| Walking | 5% | 10% |

Describe the main features and make comparisons. The values describe a fictional campus, not research data. In a normal rotating block write an overview and one comparison paragraph; reserve a full response of at least 150 words for a separate 20-minute attempt.

Original Task 2-style prompt: **Some people think universities should make work placements compulsory for every student. Others believe students should choose how to use their study time. Discuss both views and give your own opinion.** Use a planning or paragraph drill in a short block. A complete attempt needs at least 250 words and about 40 minutes, outside the normal daily block.
