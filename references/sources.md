Official format references:

- [Listening](https://ielts.org/take-a-test/test-types/ielts-academic-test/ielts-academic-format-listening): approximately 30 minutes, four parts, 40 questions; a scored first attempt uses one hearing.
- [Academic Reading](https://ielts.org/take-a-test/test-types/ielts-academic-test/ielts-academic-format-reading): 60 minutes, three sections, 40 questions. Use passage evidence and the specific question instructions.
- [Academic Writing](https://ielts.org/take-a-test/test-types/ielts-academic-test/ielts-academic-format-writing): 60 minutes; Task 1 at least 150 words, Task 2 at least 250; roughly 20/40 minutes.
- [Speaking](https://ielts.org/take-a-test/test-types/ielts-academic-test/ielts-academic-format-speaking): 11–14 minutes, three parts; Part 2 includes one minute preparation and one to two minutes speaking.

Mini exercises follow these task types where appropriate but are not validated or calibrated tests. Practice can include replay, scaffolding, and revision after the first attempt if the changed conditions are recorded.
