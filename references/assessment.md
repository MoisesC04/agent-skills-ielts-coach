# Assessment rules

Consult the current [official scoring explanation](https://ielts.org/take-a-test/your-results/ielts-scoring-in-detail), [Writing descriptors](https://ielts.org/cdn/ielts-downloadable-assets/ielts-guidance-and-support/ielts-guides/ielts-writing-band-descriptors.pdf), and [Speaking descriptors](https://ielts.org/cdn/ielts-downloadable-assets/ielts-guidance-and-support/ielts-guides/ielts-speaking-band-descriptors.pdf) before offering numerical estimates. These sources were checked on 2026-10-23. They are linked, not reproduced in full.

## Objective results

Mark Reading or Listening only against the identified source key and instructions, recording item IDs, correct/total, answer validity, and conditions. Follow that task's rules for spelling, word limits, numbers, and accepted alternatives. Do not assume fuzzy matches or synonyms are accepted. Distinguish tutor-verified marking from a learner-reported result.

A four-item reading exercise or seven-item listening task yields a raw fraction only. Do not extrapolate it to /40, an IELTS band, an overall band, or CEFR. Full official practice section conversions may be reported only with the relevant published conversion and the caveat that thresholds vary by test version. An official score is only one issued by the testing provider.

## Writing judgments

Use Task Achievement for Academic Task 1 or Task Response for Task 2, plus Coherence and Cohesion, Lexical Resource, and Grammatical Range and Accuracy. Apply the descriptors to the actual task and response, quoting small evidence spans from the learner. Criteria are equally weighted within a task; Task 2 carries twice Task 1's weight. Do not fabricate the missing task to produce a combined Writing result.

A paragraph or outline gets qualitative feedback. A suitable complete task may receive a **provisional practice range**, with criterion evidence, limitations, and a confidence label. Do not claim examiner authority or use unjustified decimal precision. A rewrite with coaching is different evidence from an independent timed attempt.

## Speaking judgments

Use Fluency and Coherence, Lexical Resource, Grammatical Range and Accuracy, and Pronunciation, equally weighted when a sufficiently representative audio sample supports them. A transcript does not reveal acoustic fluency, stress, rhythm, intonation, or intelligibility. With text/transcript alone, discuss visible language and organization; leave pronunciation and a complete Speaking band unassessed.

Even with audio, a short or poor-quality sample may be insufficient. State the evidence boundary and offer a provisional range only when justified. Include modality, audio accessibility and quality, scope, source criteria, confidence, and what evidence would reduce uncertainty.

## CEFR and diagnostic limits

CEFR C1 is the long-term aim. Do not convert a short practice result into a CEFR label, equate a particular IELTS band mechanically with C1, or promise improvement within twelve weeks. Report observable abilities, recurring gaps, and the next testable step.
