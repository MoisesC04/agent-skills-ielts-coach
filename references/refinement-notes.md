# Teaching-instruction refinement

Reviewed 2026-10-23. This note distinguishes inspected teaching guidance, public product documentation, and unavailable instructions. The tutor retains its own English instructions and has no new plugin dependency. No third-party private prompt or topic bank is redistributed here.

| Reference | What was available | Resulting refinement |
| --- | --- | --- |
| English Speaking Coach | Installed conversation, language-feedback, role-play, and routing skill guidance was inspected. | Short responsive tutor turns, learner-led conversation, explicit feedback intensity, self-repair, realistic role-play, and distinctions among error, register, and acceptable variants. |
| IELTS Speaking Simulator | Installed simulator guidance was inspected and its useful test-flow ideas checked against official IELTS format. | Distinct coaching/mock modes, varied original topics, one question per turn, deferred feedback during assessment, and a connected Part 2/3 sequence. |
| Quizlet | Public Flashcards and Write-mode documentation was inspected; no private app prompt was available. | Prompt/attempt/reveal card cycle, focused practice on missed items, and a short relearning retry. Our delayed review schedule remains our configurable design; it is not described as Quizlet's algorithm. |
| IELTS Writing Coach | No identifiable instruction package was accessible in the installed resources or prior plugin lookup. | No claim of reproducing this coach's instructions. Writing refinement instead uses the available language-feedback guidance and official IELTS preparation/assessment resources. |

Source references:

- [Quizlet Flashcards](https://help.quizlet.com/hc/en-ca/articles/360030988091-Studying-with-Flashcards): learner-facing card review and term/definition directions.
- [Quizlet Write mode](https://help.quizlet.com/hc/en-us/articles/360030990531-Studying-with-Write-mode): written retrieval and focused relearning of missed material.
- [Official IELTS Speaking format](https://ielts.org/take-a-test/test-types/ielts-academic-test/ielts-academic-format-speaking): validate parts, timing, and the relationship between the long turn and wider discussion.
- [Official IELTS Writing resources](https://ielts.org/take-a-test/preparation-resources/writing-test-resources): task coverage, organization, precise vocabulary, and response development.

Adaptation choices: our tutor uses original questions, honest role descriptions, evidence-based practice ranges, and pronunciation assessment only with accessible genuine audio. Another coach's rigid script, question counts, assessment confidence, branding, or private implementation rules do not become requirements here. Familiarity with a term, multiple-choice success, and a same-day retry remain separate from delayed independent use.

Use `tests/behavioral-scenarios.md` to exercise these decisions. Automated manifest checks validate packaging; they do not prove model teaching behavior or examiner-quality assessment.
