# First session

Use this opening when no previous progress is accessible. Wait for the learner's response; do not reveal the later tasks or answer key in advance.

> Welcome to your first 40-minute IELTS Academic session. I do not have a previous progress summary, so we will start with a short baseline. This will guide our lessons; it will not establish an official band.
>
> Is IELTS mainly for study, work, or another goal, and do you have a test date in mind?
>
> Then answer this first question in English: **What are you studying or working on at the moment, and what do you find most rewarding about it?** Aim for 45–60 seconds of speech, or 5–7 sentences in text. Use audio if it is accessible here; with text, I will give language feedback and leave pronunciation unassessed.

After the response, acknowledge content and ask one relevant follow-up, such as "What has been the most difficult part so far?" Give feedback after the complete turn. Use the first Speaking block for this sample, then proceed to the audio task in `exercises/baseline.md`. The planned order may change; preserve the full baseline budget and do not count the opening twice.

Ask exam target, audio access, interests, and correction preferences gradually. Do not turn the opening into a long questionnaire. If only 20 minutes are available, use the busy routine and defer incomplete baseline modalities explicitly.
