# IELTS Listening

Use English. First check accessible progress for a due listening pattern or missing audio evidence. If none is available, start an official short sample without assuming a level.

1. Choose an official task with a functioning audio player and matching questions/key. Start at the [IELTS Academic samples](https://ielts.org/take-a-test/preparation-resources/sample-test-questions/academic-test), preferably Note Completion. [British Council samples](https://takeielts.britishcouncil.org/prepare/ielts-free-practice-mock-tests/academic/listening) are a fallback. Link the actual task and keep provider material on its site.
2. Confirm the learner can hear the audio. Access to a transcript or a Play button does not prove learner playback or that you can hear the audio. If neither source plays, mark Listening blocked and schedule it next; never present reading a fabricated transcript as Listening.
3. Give the provider's item range and instructions, including word/number limits. Ask for numbered answers. Keep the key and transcript hidden until the attempt. For first-attempt assessment use one hearing; record any replays or assistance.
4. Retrieve the exact source key after submission. Give correct/total only for the attempted set and identify errors with source evidence. If the key is unavailable, leave marking pending or label a learner-reported result. Do not invent answers from an unheard clip.
5. Review one or two causes: missed detail, distractor, paraphrase, spelling, or instruction handling. Replay relevant audio after recording first-attempt conditions, then retry an unseen item or summarize the contrast.
6. Record source, item IDs, modality, raw result, conditions, error/correction, and next retrieval in a private session summary. Partial sets do not support band conversion or CEFR assessment.

Full IELTS Listening is approximately 30 minutes with four parts and 40 items; daily excerpts are training. Consult the [official format](https://ielts.org/take-a-test/test-types/ielts-academic-test/ielts-academic-format-listening) if rules are uncertain. In this repository, read `references/sources.md` for tested links and `templates/session-log.md` for export. In a host without these files, use the direct official links and display a concise private summary.
