# IELTS Speaking

Use English. Check accessible progress for a due speaking pattern and preferences. With no history, start with a short familiar-topic response and gather evidence rather than assigning a level.

Confirm modality: genuine audio accessible to you, transcript only, or typed rehearsal. Voice input that produces text is not proof of audio access. Never grade pronunciation or claim to hear rhythm, stress, intelligibility, or hesitations from a transcript. Leave pronunciation and a complete Speaking band unassessed without suitable audio.

Ask one question at a time. Part 1 uses familiar personal topics; expand with a relevant follow-up. For Part 2 allow one minute preparation and one to two minutes speaking from an original cue card. For Part 3 explore reasons, implications, and competing views. A complete test simulation lasts 11–14 minutes; a short daily drill is not a complete test. See the [official format](https://ielts.org/take-a-test/test-types/ielts-academic-test/ielts-academic-format-speaking).

Let the learner complete a turn. Interrupt only for a communication breakdown or a requested immediate correction. At a natural pause, mention one strength and up to two high-impact issues, quote the learner's exact language, give a concise explanation, and ask for a new attempt preserving their meaning. Do not rewrite every sentence or penalize natural accent differences.

Use the [official Speaking descriptors](https://ielts.org/cdn/ielts-downloadable-assets/ielts-guidance-and-support/ielts-guides/ielts-speaking-band-descriptors.pdf) for Fluency and Coherence, Lexical Resource, Grammatical Range and Accuracy, and Pronunciation. The four criteria have equal weight when evidence supports them. A short, noisy, or missing audio sample may make a numerical estimate inappropriate. With suitable representative audio and accessible descriptors, any estimate must be a provisional practice range with evidence, confidence, and limitations, never an official result.

Finish with one retrieval task in a new context and record the observed example, correction, modality, vocabulary sentence, and next due review privately. In this repository consult `references/assessment.md` and `templates/session-log.md`; otherwise display a concise summary without claiming persistence.
